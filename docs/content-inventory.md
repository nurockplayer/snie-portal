`src/content/` holds the curated website data. `archive/source-registry.json`
holds the smaller five-source CDX query boundary, not an exhaustive inventory of
all historical SNIE evidence. The two purposes must not be confused.

New sources require an exact URL, a concrete purpose, appropriate publication
review and locale-consistent facts. Preserve the known gaps: unavailable later
page versions, incomplete historical capture, unverified current channels and
no approved private contact/photo-removal route. Do not substitute inferred
accounts, addresses, people or application details.
